# Cast-Iron Pizza Dough — 10-inch + 12-inch Lodge

**Mixer:** KitchenAid Classic K45SS with dough hook  
**Yield:** 1 × 10-inch Lodge pizza + 1 × 12-inch Lodge pizza  
**Yeast:** Active dry yeast

## Equipment

- KitchenAid Classic K45SS mixer
- Dough hook
- Large bowl for rising
- Bowl cover, plastic wrap, or clean kitchen towel
- 10-inch Lodge cast-iron skillet
- 12-inch Lodge cast-iron skillet
- Measuring cups and measuring spoons
- Kitchen scale (optional, useful for dividing dough)
- Oven capable of reaching 500°F

## Ingredients

- 4 cups bread flour (about 520 g)
- 1 1/2 cups warm water (360 g / 105–110°F)
- 1 1/2 tsp active dry yeast
- 2 tsp sugar
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- Additional olive oil for the cast-iron pans

## Steps

1. **Bloom the yeast:** Add the warm water, sugar, and active dry yeast to the KitchenAid bowl. Stir briefly and let sit for **5–10 minutes**, until foamy.
2. Add the **3 tbsp olive oil**.
3. Add the flour and salt.
4. Attach the dough hook. Mix on **Speed 2 for about 2 minutes**, until combined.
5. Continue kneading on **Speed 2 for 7–8 minutes**. The dough should be smooth, soft, and slightly tacky.
6. Place the dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover, and let rise for **1–1 1/2 hours**, or until roughly doubled.
7. Divide the dough for the two pans, giving the **12-inch Lodge about 60%** of the dough and the **10-inch Lodge about 40%**. Shape into balls and rest for **10 minutes**.
8. Generously oil both cast-iron pans. Use about **2–3 tbsp olive oil in the 12-inch pan** and **1 1/2–2 tbsp in the 10-inch pan**.
9. Place each dough ball in its pan and gently press it toward the edges. If the dough springs back, wait **10 minutes** and continue pressing.
10. Cover the pans and let the dough rise in the pans for **30–45 minutes**, until puffy.
11. Preheat the oven to **500°F** with a rack in the lower-middle position.
12. Add sauce, cheese, and toppings. Avoid overloading the pizzas with wet toppings.
13. Bake until the tops are bubbling and the bottoms are deeply golden and crisp, roughly **12–16 minutes**. Start checking the 10-inch pizza first.
14. Remove the pizzas from the pans shortly after baking and place on a rack or board so the bottoms stay crisp.

## Notes

- The **1 1/2 tsp active dry yeast** is the standard amount for this version.
- This dough is wetter than the pizza-stone dough, helping produce a softer, airier interior in the cast-iron pans.
- For pizza tomorrow, the dough can also be refrigerated after kneading and brought back toward room temperature before dividing and panning.
